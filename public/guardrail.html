PRODUCT CASE STUDY

A guardrail for the last mile of localization.

Clearlyy helps teams communicate across languages. This prototype explores the quality layer between translated content and the frontline worker who acts on it.

01 · PROBLEM

Small translation changes can become operational risk.

A missing “not,” changed duration, or mistranslated action can alter how work is performed.

02 · APPROACH

Rules for certainty, AI for meaning.

Deterministic checks verify numbers, units, negations, terms, and structure. A free LLM reviews semantic fidelity.

03 · WORKFLOW

Evidence before publication.

Reviewers see exact source and target excerpts, confidence, suggested action, a saved audit trail, and a downloadable report.

04 · COST

Designed for a zero-cost pilot.

Browser-side checks handle obvious risks. Groq’s free tier is used only for meaning-level review, with safe fallback when unavailable.
